BEFORE YOU START

STATIC uses intense sound design as its primary tool. Specifically:

  • Low-frequency effects. Deep sub-bass pressure you may feel more than hear. Some people find this physically uncomfortable.
  • Sudden loud moments. Short, sharp sounds at close range, including jump-scare-style stings.
  • Long stretches of near-silence, used deliberately. If the game goes quiet, it has not crashed.
  • Wide dynamic range. Quiet passages are genuinely quiet. Set your volume during a loud moment, not a quiet one.

You can change any of this at any time in Settings. Two controls are worth knowing now:

Headphones are recommended, but the game is mixed to still work on laptop and phone speakers.

FIELD NOTES — INCIDENT 11-C

Pinebridge Station was a U.S. survey outpost, evacuated in a single night. A four-person team never signed out.

Their field recorders are still out there. Recover all eight tapes, then reach the fire-access road at the east boundary.

You have a flashlight with a finite battery — manage it. You have a body — sprint, crouch, vault, lean. You have nothing else.

Locals had a name for what the team found. They called it Palebark. It hears. It watches. If the picture starts to break up, it is close.

STATIC is an original work of interactive fiction. Any resemblance to existing properties is coincidental.
